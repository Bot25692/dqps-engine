# Official A.D.A.P.T. artwork

Derived from the official JPEG supplied by the project owner on 2026-10-08.
The artwork is cropped and resized without redrawing or recoloring it.

- `adapt-logo.webp`: full emblem and wordmark, 320 × 320, quality 86.
- `adapt-emblem.webp`: emblem-only crop, 128 × 128, quality 88.
- `app/favicon.ico`: emblem at 16, 32, 48 and 64 pixels.

Pre-compressed assets are served directly without runtime image transforms.
No image dependency was added; preparation used the existing Sharp installation.
